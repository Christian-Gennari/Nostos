# Citation Style Language files

The citation styles and locales in this folder come from the Citation Style
Language project and are used unmodified under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

Sources, as of 2026-10-21:
- styles: https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles (commit ffd2a7b9)
- locales: https://github.com/citation-style-language/locales (commit 8bef426f)

- `styles/apa.csl`: APA Style 7th edition. Authors and contributors: Brenton M. Wiernik, Andrew Dunning, Jay Pfaffman.
- `styles/chicago-author-date.csl`: Chicago Manual of Style 18th edition (author-date). Authors and contributors: Andrew Dunning.
- `styles/chicago-notes-bibliography.csl`: Chicago Manual of Style 18th edition (notes and bibliography). Authors and contributors: Andrew Dunning.
- `styles/harvard-cite-them-right.csl`: Cite Them Right 12th edition (author-date/Harvard). Authors and contributors: Patrick O'Brien.
- `styles/modern-language-association.csl`: MLA Handbook 9th edition (in-text citations). Authors and contributors: Sebastian Karcher, Andrew Dunning, Patrick O'Brien.
- `styles/oxford-guide-to-style-notes.csl`: Oxford Guide to Style (notes). Authors and contributors: Andrew Dunning.
- `locales/locales-en-US.xml`, `locales/locales-en-GB.xml`: CSL locales, by the CSL project contributors.

Citations are formatted by citeproc-js (npm `citeproc`), used under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3 or later.
