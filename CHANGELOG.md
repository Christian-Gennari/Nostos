### 📖 Reader & Notes

- Right-click an EPUB selection to add a note, or save a highlight where you marked it. ([#655](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/655))
- On desktop, the EPUB selection menu opens when you release the mouse and shows a fuller quote. ([#660](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/660))
- EPUB pages follow repeated light and dark mode changes without reloading your book. ([#662](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/662))
- Libron is the default EPUB typeface; Publisher keeps a book's original typography. ([#671](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/671))
- The concept map has clearer labels and grouped connections, with more space on phones. ([#667](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/667))
- Concept-map labels keep their full background so the words remain readable. ([#672](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/672))

### 📚 Library & Discovery

- OPDS catalogues work with readers that require the correct feed format. ([#633](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/633))
- Find a book has more room for results and details, keeps your selection steady, and handles slow catalogues. ([#654](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/654))
- Keep the search field clear of the toolbar controls. ([#664](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/664))

### 🤖 Ask Nostos

- Sources appear once, grouped by book, so references are easier to follow. ([#635](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/635))
- Ask Nostos recovers relevant book passages when a question is phrased in natural language. ([#639](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/639))
- Ask Nostos has simpler header controls and keeps replies in view as they grow. ([#644](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/644))
- Choose how Ask Nostos saves typed or spoken thoughts, and view or restore the original wording. ([#666](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/666))

### 🛠️ Improvements & Fixes

- Ask Nostos sees each book's collections, can edit descriptions, and shows one message when you stop a reply. ([#652](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/652))
- Failed file replacements keep your existing book, Ask Nostos explains unfinished work, and View settings lets you switch light and dark mode. ([#653](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/653))
- Find a book names the catalogues being searched and shows each source's status while you wait. ([#661](https://github.com/Christian-Gennari/Nostos/pull/661))
