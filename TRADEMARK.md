## 5. Rebranding a Fork

If you build and distribute a fork of the Nostos codebase under the terms of the software license, you must:

1. Replace the name `Nostos` in the application title, metadata, and documentation with your own unique name.
2. Replace all instances of the Nostos logo, brand marks, and default favicon with your own original graphics.
3. Include an attribution notice stating that your software is a fork of Nostos, without implying official association:
   > *"This product is based on code from the open-source Nostos project, but is an independent fork and is not endorsed or affiliated with Nostos or Christian Gennari."*
