# Nostos

A home for your reading life. Nostos keeps reading, thinking and writing in one place.

### Features

- One library for physical books, EPUB, PDF, TXT, MOBI and audiobooks (MP3, M4A, M4B)
- Free books built in: Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, Wikisource and LibriVox
- EPUB and PDF readers and an audiobook player, with highlights, notes and passages
- The Brain: link ideas with `[[Topic]]` and follow them back to their source
- Writing Studio: quote your notes with `@` and write in plain Markdown
- OPDS catalog for e-reader apps such as KOReader
- Local backups, and a portable `.nostos` export you can move to any Nostos

### Good to know

- There is no account to create. Self-hosted Nostos has no login of its own, so keep it on your home network or a VPN, or turn on authentication in front of it before exposing it.
- Everything Nostos keeps (database, books, backups and the key ring for AI keys) is in the app's `data` folder. Back it up.
- Books already on your server: put them in `media/data/books` in your Runtipi folder, then open **Settings → This server → External media**. Nostos reads them in place and never changes them.
- Give Nostos at least 512 MB of memory; 1 GB leaves room for large imports and backups.
